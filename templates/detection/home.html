Technology Overview

Understanding Deepfake Technology

Synthetic media generated by deep neural networks poses unprecedented challenges to digital security, misinformation, and media trust.

What Are Deepfakes?

Deepfakes are synthetic audio, video, or image media where a person's likeness, facial expression, or voice has been altered using deep learning models such as Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs).

How Are They Created?

Generators create synthetic frames while discriminators challenge them. Facial landmark swapping and diffusion models blend artificial faces into destination videos frame-by-frame.

Why Detection Matters

Automated AI verification prevents identity fraud, protects journalism credibility, and safeguards KYC onboarding systems from sophisticated synthetic presentation attacks.
