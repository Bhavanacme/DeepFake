About Deepfake Detection System

A comprehensive AI-driven forensic platform built for Diploma CSE Final-Year Project Demonstration.

Project Objective & Purpose

The primary objective of the Deepfake Detection System is to engineer a dependable, secure, and accessible platform that detects whether uploaded images and videos are authentic or artificially manipulated using deep learning.

As generative AI technologies (such as GANs, Diffusion Models, FaceSwap, and autoencoders) rapidly evolve, hyper-realistic synthesized videos can be generated with minimal effort. This project addresses the critical need for automated digital media verification, safeguarding personal authenticity, news credibility, and financial KYC systems.

The Role of AI & Machine Learning

Deep learning models extract hierarchical representations ranging from low-level pixel noise to high-level facial landmarks. Unlike human observers who can be easily deceived by seamless video blending, convolutional neural networks identify subtle mathematical inconsistencies such as unnatural eye blink frequencies, color channel misalignments, and frequency domain cutoff anomalies.

Security & Media Authenticity

Authenticity verification is a cornerstone of modern cybersecurity. By combining automated facial extraction with verifiable cryptographic hash tracking, tamper audit trails, and exportable PDF certificates, our system delivers a robust chain-of-evidence framework suitable for educational and institutional deployments.
